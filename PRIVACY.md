# LIT: Job Search Tracker — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 19, 2026_

LIT is a Chrome extension that helps you track your job search. This policy explains what data LIT handles and where it goes.

## Summary

- Your data stays in your browser. LIT has no servers of its own and does not collect, sell, or share your data.
- LIT sends data to a third party only when you turn on a feature that needs it (optional AI answers or optional Google sign-in), and only to that service.

## Data LIT stores on your device

LIT saves the following in your browser's local extension storage. It is not sent to the developer.

- **Job application log:** company, role, job link, date, and status of applications you log or that LIT detects on supported job sites.
- **Tracker counts and settings:** daily counts (for example applications or coding problems solved), goals, reminders, and preferences.
- **Autofill profile:** information you choose to enter so LIT can fill application forms, such as name, email, phone, location, links, work history, education, and saved answers to common questions.
- **Optional API key:** if you enter your own Anthropic API key for AI answers, it is stored locally and used only to call Anthropic's API.

You can delete any of this at any time from LIT's settings, or by removing the extension, which deletes all of its stored data.

## How LIT reads web pages

- On supported job application sites (such as Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, Workday and others listed in the extension), LIT reads the page to detect when you submit an application and to fill form fields you ask it to fill.
- On Google Calendar, LIT displays your tracker progress on the page. It does not read or send your calendar events.
- On LeetCode and NeetCode, LIT detects solved problems to update your practice count.
- On other sites, LIT runs only when you click it, use its right-click menu or shortcut, or grant access to that site.

This page data is processed in your browser and is not sent to the developer.

## Data sent to third parties (only when you enable it)

- **Anthropic (api.anthropic.com):** if you add your own API key and ask LIT to draft an answer, the application question and the relevant parts of your saved profile are sent to Anthropic to generate the answer. Anthropic's privacy policy applies: https://www.anthropic.com/legal/privacy
- **Google sign-in (googleapis.com):** if you choose to sign in with Google, LIT requests only your basic profile (name, email, profile picture) to identify your account. Google's privacy policy applies: https://policies.google.com/privacy

LIT does not use analytics, ads, or tracking, and does not sell or transfer your data for any purpose unrelated to the extension's features.

## Limited Use

LIT's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the [Chrome Web Store User Data Policy](https://developer.chrome.com/docs/webstore/program-policies/user-data-faq), including the Limited Use requirements.

## Children

LIT is not directed at children under 13.
