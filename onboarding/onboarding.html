Apply faster

Let LIT fill in the boring parts

Save your details once, and LIT fills them into job applications for you.

  • Your profile, saved once. Name, contact info, links, work history, and answers to common questions.
  • One click on the form. On an application, press the Autofill button at the bottom right, or Alt+Shift+F.
  • Nothing is submitted without you. You check every field and press Submit yourself. Your profile stays on this computer.

Count applications on any career site

On

LIT already counts applications on Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, Workday, and more than 20 other job sites. Turn this on to also count them on a company's own careers page.

Chrome will then ask to let LIT "read and change data on all websites." That is Chrome's standard wording. Here is what it means for LIT:

What LIT looks at

  • Whether a page is a job application
  • The job title and company when you submit

What LIT never does

  • Read your email, passwords, or other browsing
  • Send anything to a server
  • Submit a form for you

Daily check-in
